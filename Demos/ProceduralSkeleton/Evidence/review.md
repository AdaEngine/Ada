# Visual review

Reviewed the four saved native GPU frames after fixing the split forehead,
framing and upside-down key light. Reference: the supplied KayKit image.

| View | Observation |
| --- | --- |
| front.png | Complete silhouette in frame. Two luminous eyes, visible socket recesses, nasal triangle, teeth, scarf, ribs, belt, legs, sword and shield. |
| three-quarter.png | Skull depth, recess walls, jaw and thickness of equipment visible; recognizable stylized skeleton. |
| side.png | Cranium has actual volume; ribs wrap around the body. Sword and arms overlap in this projection, as expected for this pose. |
| back.png | Closed faceted rear cranium, exposed spine/ribs, scarf tail, belt, both legs and backs of props visible. |

Artistic limitations: the forehead is flatter and the side silhouette boxier than
the reference; details and proportions are approximate. Scarf folds and wood are
geometric color regions, not texture maps. Fine edges alias at this resolution.
This is a static prototype, not accepted production character art or a deformation test.
